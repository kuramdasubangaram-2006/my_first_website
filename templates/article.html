The quiet signals
inside a web request

What a browser shares by default, what it keeps behind a permission prompt, and why the distinction matters.

SDBy Signal Desk
5 min read · Fictional reporting
01THE PUBLIC LAYER

Every ordinary page load leaves a small, understandable footprint: a timestamp, a browser signature, a language preference, and the network address that delivered the request. These signals help websites work, but they are not a license to infer a person’s identity.

This demonstration separates that baseline from capabilities that require a deliberate choice. A browser does not grant a site your precise location or camera feed just because a page asked. The decision belongs to you.
